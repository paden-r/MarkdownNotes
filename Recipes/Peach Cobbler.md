### Ingredients

- 4 pounds peaches (peeled and sliced) 
- 1 cup blueberries
- ¼ cup granulated sugar  
- 1/2 cup light brown sugar  
- 4 tablespoons unsalted butter (melted)
- 2 tablespoons corn starch  
- 2.5 tablespoon lemon juice  
- 2 teaspoons lemon zest  
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract or vanilla bean paste  
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon  
- ¼ teaspoon fresh grated nutmeg  
- ¼ teaspoon allspice
- [[Cobbler Toppping]]


### Method
1. Preheat oven to 425 Degrees 
2. Add Peaches, blueberries, brown sugar, granulated sugar, cinnamon, allspice, butter, corn starch, lemon juice, lemon zest, vanilla to a large bowl and mix together
3. Place peach mixture in buttered (or greased) 9x13 pan
4. Top peach mixture with Cobbler Topping
5. Bake for 30 minutes until top is golden brown
